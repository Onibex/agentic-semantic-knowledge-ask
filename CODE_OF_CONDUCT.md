# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be straightforward and be civil. Argue with the work, not the person who did it.

## What we expect

This repository is a working space, and the standard is the one a good colleague meets:

- **Say what you mean, plainly.** Direct technical disagreement is welcome and useful.
  "This is wrong because X" is a contribution; "this is nonsense" is not.
- **Assume the other person had a reason.** Most decisions that look strange were made
  under a constraint you cannot see from the diff. Ask what it was.
- **Take correction the same way you give it.** Being wrong about something is the
  ordinary cost of working on hard problems, not a status event.
- **Keep other people's data out of it.** Redact credentials, customer names and
  business figures from issues, screenshots and logs.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments about who someone is rather than what
they wrote, sustained disruption, publishing private information, and unwelcome
attention of any kind.

This applies wherever the project happens: issues, pull requests, commit messages,
code review and any channel where you are here as a participant in ASK.

## Scope and enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for what this space is like, and will edit, hide or delete
contributions that do not meet the standard above — and will say why. Repeated or
serious breaches lead to a block.

Report a problem to **contact@onibex.com**. Reports are read by the maintainers, kept
confidential, and answered. If your report concerns a maintainer, say so — it will be
handled by someone else.

Onibex, LLC stewards this repository, and enforcement decisions are ultimately ours. We
would rather explain a decision than hide behind a policy document, so ask if one looks
wrong to you.
